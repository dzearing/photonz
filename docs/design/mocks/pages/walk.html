  1. 1

    the Photonz status icon

    the macOS menu bar. Photonz is a resident agent, so this works with nothing open

    the app's real root menu drops: capture, history, then the ways to make a document. There is no app to launch and no window yet.

  2. 2

    Capture Region, or press ⇧⌘4 from anywhere

    the Photonz menu, first group

    the screen goes to a crosshair and a selection appears over the pricing page, with its live pixel size on the edge.

  3. 3

    the selection across the pricing page and let go

    the screen itself, wherever the thing you want lives

    a capture toast slides into the corner with the real thumbnail, a green check, and Copied to clipboard! It is already pasteable, and the card leaves on its own.

  4. 4

    Show History, or press ⇧⌘H from anywhere

    the Photonz menu, second group. Both doors reach one surface

    the chromeless history pane drops over the screen with the new capture first and already selected, so its action row shows: open, copy, pin, delete.

  5. 5

    Open

    the selected card's action row, in the filmstrip

    an editor window opens onto that capture. The title bar reads pricing-page · 2560 × 1440 and the shot is the locked bottom layer. The history is still up, so you could open another.

  6. 6

    the Photonz icon again, or press ⎋, or click anywhere else

    the menu bar. The pane is chromeless, so there is no close button to hunt for

    the overlay retracts and the editor is clear: canvas, floating tool bar, one right dock of panel groups. Everything from here is the ordinary shell.

  7. 7

    the Text callout tool

    the floating tool bar, under the canvas

    Text becomes the active tool and Properties switches to its Paint: the blue callout style, 13/600 type, an 8pt radius. Tools and Properties are contextual, never a mode you hunt for.

  8. 8

    a callout above the Pro card and point it at the button

    the canvas, straight onto the capture

    the callout and its arrow land as a layer, listed above the locked screenshot, selected on the canvas, in Layers, and in Properties at once. That three-place selection is the same everywhere in the app.

  9. 9

    the Measure tool

    the floating tool bar, after the mark-up cluster

    the caliper is live, the toolbar gains a Logical (px) unit readout, and Properties shows what it will snap to. Same tool, same slot, whether you measure a photo or a button.

  10. 10

    from the Starter card's edge to the Pro card's edge

    the canvas, between the two cards

    the caliper snaps to both edges and reads 20, and a second one calls the Pro card 168 wide. Red is size, blue is spacing, and the measurements are a layer like anything else.

  11. 11

    the Callout row

    the Layers group, at the top of the right dock

    selection moves back to the callout: the ring returns on the canvas and Properties shows its Paint again. Selecting in Layers and selecting on the canvas are the same act.

  12. 12

    Make component, or press ⌥⌘K

    the Layers panel menu, behind the header's more button

    the callout becomes a main component and joins your Library. Properties now shows its variant and fill. Nothing moved on the canvas: a component is just a layer tree with a name.

  13. 13

    the Library group header

    the right dock, bottom group

    Library expands at scope Components and the new Callout tile is sitting there beside Badge and Lower third. One Library with four scopes, not a new panel per feature.

  14. 14

    the Callout tile onto the Starter card

    the Library group, scope Components, out to the canvas

    a linked instance lands, marked with the purple component ring. It is a live link to the main, not a copy, so editing the main later updates it.

  15. 15

    the Fill style, then pick Brand / gradient

    Properties, in the Component section. This is where the old floating mini inspector went

    the instance re-paints and the readout still says link to main · intact. Overrides are per instance, so you can restyle one without cutting it loose. That is the whole reuse tier: Tokens, Styles, Components.

The whole arc

Capture from the menu bar, land in history, open in the editor, mark it up, measure it, then promote the mark to a Component and drop it back. One document, one layer stack, one dock. Nothing here is a second app.

Across scenarios

Because a component is just a layer tree, the same Callout drops onto a UI frame, a photo, or a video track, re-painted with Styles and keyframed where there is time. Learn it once and it applies everywhere.

Automatable

Every step is one API call (capture.region, history.open, layer.addText, measure.between, component.make, instance.place), so an agent can run the same flow. Where is the line between guiding a person and doing it for them?
