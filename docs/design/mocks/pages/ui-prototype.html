The idea

A prototype is just states plus triggers. Point a tap target at a destination state, pick a transition, and the flow runs for real. No separate prototyping mode: the same component states you design are the frames you wire, and they sit in Layers like every other layer.

A direction

Because a connection is data, the whole graph is addressable. on("tap", "#cta").navigate("Detail", {transition:"slide", ms:240}) is exactly what the panel writes, so an agent can wire, retime, or audit a flow the same way you do.

Open question

Should a transition be able to carry per-layer motion (a hero image that persists and grows between two frames), or stay a whole-screen dissolve or slide? Shared-element motion is gorgeous but much harder to keep predictable across every wired pair.
