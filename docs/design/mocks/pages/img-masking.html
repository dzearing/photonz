The idea

A mask decides where an edit lands, without touching a pixel. Flip between a painted Layer mask, a Luminosity mask that targets highlights or shadows, a smooth Gradient mask, and an agent-selected mask locked to the sky · the warm adjustment moves with it, live.

Direction

All four are the same primitive: an alpha Paint channel on the layer. Feather reshapes the edge, Strength is the layer opacity, and Show mask paints the channel red like a quick mask. Any mask is editable by hand after the fact · the agent select is just a starting point, not a lock-in.

Open question

When two masks make sense at once, say a sky select intersected with a highlights luminosity mask, should the UI stack them as a small mask list with blend modes, or fold it into one sentence to the agent and show the combined result?
