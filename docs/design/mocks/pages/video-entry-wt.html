Open a recording and trim it

· usage clickthrough · from the blank slate

Where a video document comes from when there is nothing on screen at all. A screen recording lands in the global history exactly like a screenshot does, so the entry is: open history from the menu-bar agent, filter to Videos, and the editor is what the recording opens into, timeline and all. Trim the flubbed ending, export, done. Eight real clicks on ONE screen, which never re-renders below.

The idea

Video gets no special front door. A recording lands in the same global history as a screenshot, the same Open makes a document of it, and the only thing that changes in the editor is what the document itself brings: time, so the transport and timeline appear. Entry is identical across surfaces; the document is what differs.

A direction

The history card for a recording could offer Trim here without opening the editor at all: scrub inside the card, drag two handles, copy the result. The editor stays the place for real work; the pane handles the fast crop.

Open question

Should Export for a one-clip document default to the clipboard, like a capture does, instead of a file? Copy-to-paste is the fast daily path for screenshots; recordings may want the same.
