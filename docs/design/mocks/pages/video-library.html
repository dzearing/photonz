The Library in a video

· a catalog of things built for video, grouped by kind

With a video open, the Library has two pivots: Media (the recordings, sounds and pictures this video holds) and Catalog. Styles and Systems belong to pictures and designs and are not shown. The catalog files everything made for video under what it is, so finding a name card is a glance at a chip, not a scroll down one long list.

Placement (UX-PATTERNS, placement contract)

  • The Library is a dock group, whose purpose is things you place: the chips, the search box and the filter are that group's own controls, not the tool bar's or the timeline's.
  • Adding is where you click: double click, drag onto a track, or right-click a tile for Insert at Playhead (transitions: Apply to Every Cut, Set as Default Transition).

Kinds

  • Title Pages, Name Cards, Animated Charts, Callouts, Backgrounds, Transitions, Logos & Brand (the pictures this video holds), End Cards, and Components (only with Built for video off).
  • A kind with nothing in it has no chip: Animated Charts appears once its own task ships; Callouts and End Cards once something is made for them.

The fork

  • Chips that wrap above the shelf, or a column of kinds down the left of the shelf, or one dropdown naming the kind: this page draws chips. The other two are on the decision card for this task.
