Preview a page of components

· prototype: try the page as the real thing

For review. Today the canvas is always for editing: pointing at a copy only opens its name tag, and clicking picks it. Here, Preview makes the page in front of you behave like the real controls, using only the looks you already made, with nothing to set up. Everything below really works, so press Preview and use the page. The page is drawn in HTML; every rule about which look shows when is the one a build would run.

Nothing to set up

The looks' own names say what they are for. Default, Hover, Pressed, Focused, Disabled, On, Off, Checked and Selected are read straight off the component. A look named for words the control shows (Day, Week, Month) moves to the word you click.

A state with no look

The copy keeps the look it has. Point at the Selected tab, or at the checked box: there is no Selected Hover, so it stays put rather than flipping back to Default. Name a look On Hover and it is used, as the switch here shows.

Nothing is kept

Flip everything, then leave. The page, the selection, the zoom and Undo are exactly as you left them, because Preview never touches the document. The count above the window never moves.

The answers

· each with what is recommended and why
QuestionRecommendationWhy
The way inA Preview button at the right of the title bar, on a picture holding at least one component copy, plus View ▸ Preview and ⌘Return.The title bar's job is how you are looking at the window. ⌘Return is free, and it is the key the prototyping mock already prints for Play flow. While text is being typed it still finishes the text.
The way outEsc, ⌘Return again, or the same button.Three doors, none of them on the page, so nothing you are trying can be mistaken for a way out.
What goesTool bar, tool options, panel, Ask, name tags, selection, guides and rulers. The title bar stays, with the button lit.You are looking at the page, not working on it. Keeping the title bar keeps the way out where you pressed to come in.
The page100 percent, or fitted when it would not fit. Your zoom and scroll come back on leaving. On a canvas with several screens, the screen holding your selection, else the one most in view.A control is judged at its real size.
The pillOne small glass pill at the bottom, where the tool bar was: Preview, Esc to leave. It stays while you are in Preview.Your task asked for it. It is the one status pill allowed on the canvas, since the placement contract otherwise keeps pills off it; everything that would have explained things has gone.
Looks named otherwisePointing at one puts a short note in the pill: Plan: no state looks. Try the card. It never blocks anything.Your checklist asks for a note, not an error, and the pill is the only place that covers nothing you are trying.
TabTab and ⇧Tab move in reading order and wrap. They stop only where a Focused look can show; Space or Return is a click on what has focus.A stop that shows nothing looks like the key failed. Preview draws only your looks, so it adds no focus ring of its own.

What each kind does

· decided by the looks it has
It hasIt behaves asIn Preview
Hover, PressedA buttonPoint: Hover. Press: Pressed. Slide off while held: back, and no click. Release over it: a click, then Hover again.
On, or CheckedA switch or checkboxA click flips it and it stays. Default beside Checked counts as Off.
On, in a component named RadioA radioA click turns it on, and the others of that component in the same group turn off. A click on the one that is on does nothing.
SelectedA tabThe same: copies of one component in one group keep one selected. A lone copy flips like a switch. Copies not in a group count as the page's group.
Looks named for its own wordsA segmented controlA click on a word shows that word's look. That is how the sheets Ask makes draw them, as one component.
Focused, without Pressed or OnA fieldA click gives it focus and Focused holds until focus moves. Typing into it is not part of this step.
Disabled, as the look it showsDisabledNothing responds, and Tab passes over it.
