The idea

A transition is not a filter on a clip, it is a relationship between two clips at a cut. Pick it from the panel group, watch it run on the real frames, and drag its length right on the timeline. Show the behavior, do not just name it.

One grammar everywhere

The six behaviors are library tiles, exactly like media, components and styles. The selected transition reads the same in three places: named on the canvas, highlighted on the timeline, and open in Properties.

A direction

Every transition rides the same keyframe and easing engine as the rest of the timeline, so its length and curve are animatable and an agent can set them with one call. Duration and type are just parameters, no special-case UI.

Open question

How many transitions earn a place in v1? Cross dissolve, dip, and cut cover most real edits. Slide, push, and morph are flashy but easy to overuse. Ship the honest few, or the full shelf?
