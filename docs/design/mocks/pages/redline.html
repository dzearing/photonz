Keep it sacred

Your daily workflow. Capture, measure and annotate, then to the clipboard, one keystroke away. Creation is additive and must never slow this down.

Shared foundation

Same canvas and primitives: a measure label is text on Paint, a redaction is a blur Effect. Every number here maps to a real distance on the shot.

One selection, three places

The highlighted caliper on the capture, the selected row in Measurements, and the Properties group are the same object. Measuring reuses the app's selection model instead of inventing one.
