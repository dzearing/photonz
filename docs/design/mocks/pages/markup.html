Markup

· arrows, chips and measurements on a picture

The marks a redliner draws on a screenshot are one system. An arrow and every measurement (size, distance, gap, alignment) carry the same chip, take one colour, and are set from the same Appearance. A chip has a style, a marker and a size; an arrow also has a line style. The plate behind the words is derived from the colour so the words always read. Rules: docs/design/markup.md. Component: shared/components/markup.css + markup.js.

Any colour, not just these: the rule holds for every hue, saturation and lightness (5,472 tested, worst 4.80:1). Pick a custom colour above and every chip follows. Nobody needs Advanced to stay legible.

A mid or dark ink is darkened just enough for white words to reach 4.8:1. A light ink, whose darkening would pass 22 percent, keeps its colour and takes dark words. Hairline's ink-coloured words are the ink darkened to 4.5:1 against white. Every row below is computed by PZ.markup.plateFor, the same rule the app must follow.

Markers

accent (the style's own stripe or dot), none, or a status icon

Status colours keep their meaning on any ink. On an ink-filled plate the icon sits on a white disc with its colour in the glyph; on a light or graphite plate it is the coloured disc with a white glyph (dark on yellow). Any marker but Accent takes the stripe or dot away.

The same controls sit on a picked mark (what this one is) and in the tool's options (what the next one is), for the Arrow tool and every Measure mode. A measurement has no Arrow row. Under Advanced each part reads "from Color" or "from style" until set by hand, and a part set by hand stays overridden until reset. Ending is offered only for Clean, since the hand-made styles draw their own heads.
