  1. 1

    the Photonz status icon

    the macOS menu bar. Photonz is a resident agent, so this works with no window open

    the app's real root menu drops: capture, history, then the ways to make a document. Nothing is running yet, and nothing needs to be.

  2. 2

    Capture Region, or press ⇧⌘4 from anywhere

    the Photonz menu, first group

    the screen dims to a crosshair and a selection appears over the window, with its live pixel size on the edge. No dialog, no app to launch first.

  3. 3

    the selection across the Billing window and let go

    the screen itself, wherever the thing you want lives

    a capture toast slides into the bottom right holding the real thumbnail, a green check, and Copied to clipboard! The shot is already pasteable. It is transient, so there is nothing to dismiss.

  4. 4

    Show History, or press ⇧⌘H from anywhere

    the Photonz menu, second group. Same surface either way

    the chromeless history pane drops over the screen with the new capture first and already selected, so its action row is showing: open, copy, pin, delete. This pane is the app's front door, not a panel inside a window.

  5. 5

    Open

    the selected card's action row, in the filmstrip

    an editor window opens onto that capture. The title bar reads settings-capture · 2560 × 1440 and the shot is the locked bottom layer. The history is still up, so you could open another.

  6. 6

    the Photonz icon again, or press ⎋, or click anywhere else

    the menu bar. The pane is chromeless, so there is no close button to hunt for

    the overlay retracts and the editor is clear: canvas, floating tool bar, one right dock. From here on it is the ordinary shell.

  7. 7

    the Box tool

    the floating tool bar, under the canvas

    Box becomes the active tool and Properties switches to its Paint: the blue callout style, a 1.5px stroke, a 10pt radius. Same three places, same panel, as anything else you select.

  8. 8

    a box over the Plan row, then drop a note and an arrow

    the canvas, straight onto the capture

    three marks land as three real layers, listed above the locked screenshot. Nothing is baked into the pixels, so every one of them is still draggable, restylable, and undoable.

  9. 9

    the Redact tool

    the floating tool bar, next to Text

    Properties shows the redaction Effect: mode Blur, its radius, and the fact that it is applied at render. It is an effect on a layer, never a brush that eats pixels.

  10. 10

    over the card number field

    the canvas, on top of the sensitive value

    a blur Effect covers the field and a Redacted badge marks it. The original pixels stay in the document but never leave it, so anything you copy or share is already safe.

  11. 11

    Copy image, or press ⌘C

    the command menu, under the … button at the top right of the canvas. The shipping app also has it in the native Edit menu

    the finished, redacted image goes on the clipboard, ready to paste into a ticket or a chat. Capture to paste is a handful of seconds and never leaves the keyboard.

The idea

The fast daily path is the one that must never get slower. It runs entirely through the four shell surfaces: the menu-bar agent starts the capture, the toast confirms it is already on your clipboard, history keeps it, and the editor is only where you go when you want to mark it up. Creation features are additive and sit on top of this, never in front of it.

A direction

Because every mark is a real layer, a whole redline pass could be one saved Style set, or a Component: your standard callout, your blur preset, your caliper color, dropped onto a fresh capture in one click.

Open question

Should redaction be irreversible on export by default, so a shared image can never leak the original pixels even if someone opens the source document later?
