The idea

Compositing is just layers with a rule for how they combine. Keying makes a colour transparent, a matte borrows another layer's shape or brightness, and a blend mode sets the math. One live preview shows all three reacting as you touch the controls, so the operation is felt, not read.

Every rule is a property

There is no keying mode and no matte tool. Select the layer and the rule that applies to it opens in Properties, the same place a caption style or a clip speed opens. One selection, three places: outlined on the canvas, highlighted in Layers, open in Properties.

A direction

Because a matte is just another layer, any component can be the matte source. Drop a keyframed shape on the matte track and the reveal animates for free on the same timeline the rest of the project uses.

Open question

Auto key can sample the dominant background colour and set a sane threshold in one call. Do we surface spill kill and light-wrap as defaults, or keep the first pass to a single Key it button and let power users open the rest?
