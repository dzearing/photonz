Zoom in on a detail over time

· usage clickthrough · one scenario

One job: start wide, punch in on the button, and let it hold. The move is two keys on Scale and two on Centre, and it is built with the same three gestures as every other animation in Photonz: move the playhead, change the thing, let the change be recorded. Eight real clicks on ONE screen, which never re-renders below.

  1. 1

    the screen-recording clip

    the V1 track

    the clip outlines on the timeline, a ring lands on the frame, and Properties shows a Reframe section: Scale and Centre. There is no crop mode and no separate effect. Where you are looking is a property of the clip.

  2. 2

    the playhead to 0:03, just before the click you want to show

    the transport scrubber

    this is where the camera should still be wide. A move needs a starting value, and a starting value is a key at a time.

  3. 3

    the keyframe button on the Scale row, still at 100%

    Properties, in the Reframe section

    a key at 0:03 holding 100%. Keying a value you have not changed feels odd for a second, and it is the whole trick: you are nailing down where the move begins so it cannot creep backwards.

  4. 4

    the playhead to 0:05

    the transport scrubber

    two seconds is a comfortable punch. Long enough to read as a move, short enough that nobody waits for it.

  5. 5

    the Scale value up to 220%

    Properties, in the Reframe section

    the canvas punches in live as you drag, and the second key writes itself at 0:05. The Scale lane on the timeline now shows the span between wide and tight: that span is the move.

  6. 6

    the picture until the button sits in the middle of the frame

    the canvas

    dragging the picture is Centre, and Centre keys itself the same way Scale did. Its own lane appears under the Scale lane, because a punch-in is really two properties travelling together.

  7. 7

    Ease in-out

    Properties, under Between the keys

    the punch stops arriving at a constant rate. It leans in and settles, which is the difference between a camera move and a zoom slider being dragged.

  8. 8

    the playhead on to 0:08

    the transport scrubber

    three seconds past the last key and the frame has not moved: after its final key a property just holds. That is the hold, and you did not have to ask for it. To pull back out, key 100% again wherever you want the move to land.

The idea

A punch-in is not a zoom tool. It is Scale and Centre with keys on them, the same two properties you would animate on a title or a piece of clip art. Learning it once means you have learned every move in the app.

A direction

Most punch-ins point at something the app can already find: a click, a cursor, a window. Punch in on the pointer could write these keys for you from the recording’s own click track, and leave them fully editable.

Open question

220% of a 1080p source is soft. Should the canvas warn when a punch-in passes the source resolution, and should Export quietly render from the original capture at full size when it can?
