The idea

Everything you lay over footage is a layer with an in and an out. Words, art, and a component instance differ in what they draw, not in how you place, style, move, or trim them. That is why there is no separate titler, no shape mode, and no clip-art browser.

A direction

Tracks appear when something needs one and could disappear when the last clip leaves, which keeps a simple cut simple. The open risk is a track quietly vanishing under you while you are dragging.

Open question

A title and its clip art almost always travel together. Should selecting both and pressing ⌘G make a component on the spot, so the pair becomes reusable the moment it exists?
