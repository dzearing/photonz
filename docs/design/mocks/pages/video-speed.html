The idea

Retiming is one speed curve over the clip's timeline, not a pile of separate slow-mo, freeze and fast-forward tools. Drag a stop to any speed, ease between stops for a ramp, drop a stop to zero for a held frame. One mental model covers every case.

Where the controls live

Speed is a property of the selected clip, so the stop presets, the interpolation switch, and add or delete sit in the Properties group next to every other property. The curve stays on the timeline, where time lives.

A direction

The curve rides the same keyframe engine as opacity, position and transitions on the video timeline. A component or caption can be retimed the same way, and audio can follow the speed so pitch stays sane.

An open question

Frame blending sells slow-mo, but real optical-flow interpolation is heavy. Do we ship nearest-frame slow-mo in v1 and leave smooth optical flow for the funded timeline chapter, or hold speed back until it looks premium?
