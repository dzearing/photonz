Put a transition on a cut

· usage clickthrough · one scenario

One job: make the cut between two clips a dissolve, then change it to a dip to black. The interesting part is not the picker, it is where the frames come from. A dissolve has to show both clips at once, so it needs an overlap, and an overlap is paid for with spare media past a clip's in or out point. A dip to black needs no overlap at all: each clip fades inside its own time. Photonz never moves a clip to make either of those work unless you ask, and it shows you the bill before you agree to it. Ten real clicks on ONE screen, which never re-renders below.

Your positions are never spent without you saying so.

The idea

A transition is an interval where two clips are both on screen, not an object that sits between them. So it is drawn over the seam, and it costs spare media rather than position. An untouched cut draws nothing and takes no width, because nothing is there. The only thing here that ever moves a clip is a hold on black, and that is a number you choose.

A direction

Spare media could be visible all the time as a hairline past every clip edge, so you could see at a glance which cuts in a forty-clip edit can take a dissolve. The risk is a timeline that looks busy when transitions are the last thing on your mind, which is why here it appears only on the selected cut.

Open question

With no spare, the choices are shorten the cut, use a dip, or go back and re-trim. Should there be a fourth: hold the last frame for the length of the dissolve, so outro freezes for half a second instead of moving? It costs no position and no media, and on a title card nobody would ever see it.
