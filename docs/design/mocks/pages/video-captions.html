The idea

Auto-captions land as a real track with word-level timing. Each word sits on its own time span, the current word lights up in the track and in the preview as the playhead moves, and the whole line is a Type Style you can restyle without touching a single pixel.

Captions are a tool, not a mode

Nothing about the shell changes to caption a video. You pick Title / Text in the tool bar, its options appear at the top of Properties, and the cues appear on the timeline. One selected cue reads the same in three places: ringed on the canvas, highlighted in Layers, and open in Properties.

Safe by default

Title-safe and action-safe guides keep words off the crop no matter the aspect. Broadcast wants 90% and 93%; social reels want a taller inset. The guide set is a preset an agent can pick per platform.

Open question

Word timing is easy to generate but tedious to hand-fix. Do we ship an inline nudge on each word chip, or trust re-transcription plus a global offset? The first is precise, the second is faster for most cuts.
