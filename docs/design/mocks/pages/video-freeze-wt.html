The idea

A freeze is a clip whose in and out are the same frame. Nothing new was invented for it, which is why it trims, moves, takes a transition, and carries annotations exactly like footage does.

A direction

Holding a frame to point at something is the screenshot workflow arriving in video. The natural next step is Measure on a held frame: freeze, dimension the gap, and the label rides the hold.

Open question

Picture only lets sound and image drift on purpose. Should the timeline show that drift, with an offset badge on the pair, or does that clutter the common case where nobody cares?
