Transform modes

· prototype: size, skew, 3D with perspective, distort

For review, building on Selection · Proposal 03b. The chip by the rotate knob now has three choices: Size, 3D and Distort. Perspective belongs to the document's one camera, so changing it moves every 3D layer (two cards here to show it). Keys follow Photoshop. Try them on the card.

PERSPECTIVE · THE CAMERA
