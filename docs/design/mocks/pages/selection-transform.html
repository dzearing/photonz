Transform modes

· prototype: size, skew, 3D with perspective, distort

For review, building on Selection · Proposal 03b. The chip by the rotate knob has three choices: Size, 3D and Skew & distort. Size only resizes and turns; slanting lives in its own mode. Perspective belongs to the document's one camera, so changing it moves every 3D layer (two cards here to show it). Try them on the card.
