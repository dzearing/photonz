📸

Always one keystroke away

Photonz lives in your menu bar. Grab a region (⌘⇧4) or the whole screen (⌘⇧3), slide down your capture history (⌘⇧H), pin a shot to float over everything, and open as many editor windows as you need.

📐

Redline like a pro

The measure tool (I) drops dimension lines with automatic pixel labels, and its corners snap to the UI edges in your screenshot — text baselines, buttons, borders. Spacing audits in seconds, not minutes.

🎯

Zoom callouts

Drag over any detail (Z) and Photonz flies in a magnified callout with leader lines — rectangle or circle, any magnification. The signature move for docs and bug reports.

🪄

Select regions, Photoshop-style

Box, ellipse, and magic-wand selections (M, W) with ⇧ add / ⌥ subtract / ⇧⌥ intersect. Fill them, delete to slice a layer, drag to move the pixels, or ⌘J them onto their own layer.

🎥

Record your screen

Region or full screen (⌘⇧5), with your choice of microphone. Trim and crop the result, then export MP4, GIF, or animated HEIC — ready for a PR description or a bug ticket.

🖊️

Annotate anything

Arrows, shapes, highlights, and rich text with full font control — in your current color, with a Photoshop-style foreground/background pair and paint bucket (G). Everything stays editable after the fact.

🧅

Real layers & collage

A full layers panel with non-destructive styling — blur, shadows, borders, rounded corners, opacity. One-click blur-behind, and a collage layer that arranges photos into page layouts you can reflow by dragging.

📤

Ship it anywhere

Copy the finished composite (⇧⌘C) or export PNG, JPEG, or HEIC at 1× or 2×. Documents save as editable .photonz packages, and edited captures round-trip back into your history.

⚡️

Native speed

Pure Swift with Metal-accelerated Core Image. A 12-megapixel, 10-layer document re-renders in single-digit milliseconds — every drag, every keystroke, under one frame.

First launch: builds are Developer ID signed; if macOS still warns (notarization pending), right-click the app and choose Open once — after that it launches normally.
