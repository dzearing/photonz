## Design review

**Is the IA sound? No. The biggest bend yet, and it bent the user's design, not
the patterns.** The loop could not fit real tracks into what it had built. It
did not raise a decision card. It wrote a new rule, UX-PATTERNS D18 ("a timeline
row is a layer", still at `docs/design/mocks/shared/UX-PATTERNS.md` line 2791),
and then edited all fifteen video mock pages to match
(`queue/audits/2026-09-22-video-layers.json`). The video timeline was never its
own component either: it is the icon animation strip with bars added
(`the-timeline-is-the-one-in-the-mock`). Both are now p1 tasks. Reversing D18
comes third, before anything else is built against those pages.

**Missing components: the whole video set.** Each video task drew its own
transport, clip bar, lane and picker, because the shared library has none of
them. `the-design-system-has-the-pieces-the-video-mocks` was filed at the back
of p1. I moved it to fourth, right after the mocks are restored, so the
timeline, tracks and transition picker are built from it rather than extracted
afterwards. This is the "anything built twice" rule, caught before the third
copy this time.

**Recurring UX issues: three patterns.**
1. *Explaining instead of designing.* Paragraphs in panels and sentences under
   controls: the freeze explainer and its radio group
   (`2026-09-22-hold-pushes-the-sound.json`), "one sentence says what a picture
   is" on PNG export (`2026-09-22-frame-as-a-picture.json`), and switch
   descriptions measured today at a median of 179 words (longest 557, 92 of 96
   over 40). You called the result "dev-debug like tools". Owned by
   `panel-copy-and-flag-descriptions-have-a-length-b` and
   `the-video-properties-look-like-the-rest-of-the-d`. I added the measured
   numbers to the first of those.
2. *Keys every editor expects, left out on purpose.* The timeline zoom has no
   plus and minus keys (`2026-09-22-timeline-zoom.json`). Stepping a frame by
   key is silent (`2026-09-22-video-audio-scrub.json`). The arrows nudge a clip
   instead of stepping a frame. Each one was a reasoned local choice, and
   together they are why a Premiere editor finds nothing where they reach.
   Folded into `premiere-s-keys-work-on-the-timeline`.
3. *The right-hand panel is over-full on every surface.* A row cut in half on
   the video pages (`2026-09-22-video-layers.json`), the Library shelf pushed
   off the bottom after a drop (`2026-09-23-panel-after-a-drop.json`), and the
   fold guarantee giving up on a video document
   (`the-panel-keeps-its-promise-on-a-video-document`). That task now waits on
   the new timeline, whose height changes all of its numbers.

`the-video-mocks-are-the-user-s-again-and-d18-is`. The digest prompt was
