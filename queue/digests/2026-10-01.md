## Design review
**Is the IA sound?** Yes. The three recording features fit the placement contract without bending it. One-shot verbs (Add Click at Playhead, Add Zoom, Suggest Zooms, Hide This Click) sit on the right-click menus of the thing they act on and in the Clip menu. Tuning (the Clicks switch, Style, Color, Size) sits in Properties as label and value rows. Timing sits on the timeline as a lane and ticks. Evidence: `queue/audits/2026-09-30-zoom-regions.json`, `2026-09-30-click-effects.json`, `2026-09-30-recorded-clicks.json`. One watch item: the Clicks rows add height to a recording's Properties, which is exactly the space the open card on `the-panel-keeps-its-promise-on-a-video-document` is about.

**Missing components or guidance.** The lifting row drag is about to exist twice. The layers list has its own model for rows of one height, and the half-built track drag (stash 9af77fa9) adds a second one for rows of different heights. That is folded into `timeline-tracks-reorder-by-dragging-their-header` as "make it one model, and let the layers list use it", rather than filed as a separate task. Nothing else was hand-rolled this cycle that the design system should own.

**Recurring UX issues.** Two patterns:
- **Selection chrome stacks up when a new kind of thing becomes pickable.** A picked zoom shows its own box and the clip's handles at once, with no pointer change and no Escape (`framing-a-zoom-shows-one-box-and-the-pointer-say`, p2). The click ticks repeat half of it: the canvas does not preview while you drag a tick. Both come from one place: something new inside a clip becomes pickable without taking over the canvas from the clip.
- **Edits made inside a clip ignore its crop and rotation.** Follow Cursor follows a little off on a cropped clip, and a click effect lands off target on a cropped or rotated clip. Both are written down in their audits. Neither is filed yet, because both are rare. If either turns up a second time it earns a task.

## Ready to try

- **Zoom regions.** Open a recording and switch to Edit. Put the playhead where something happens, right-click the clip and choose Add Zoom. Drag the box on the picture, pull a corner to go in further, then drag the bar's ends on the Zoom lane. Right-click the bar and choose Follow Cursor, then scrub. `queue/audits/2026-09-30-zoom-regions.json`
- **Click effects.** Click a recording on the canvas and turn on Clicks at the top of its Properties. Pick Ripple, Pulse or Spotlight and play through a click. Drag or hide a tick on the clip bar. Best on a recording made today, which has real clicks in it. `queue/audits/2026-09-30-click-effects.json`
- **Recorded clicks.** Record a region for a few seconds and click a few times in it. The clicks come back when you open it. Right-click a clip, then Add Click at Playhead, to add one by hand. `queue/audits/2026-09-30-recorded-clicks.json`
