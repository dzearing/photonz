## Design review

**Is the IA sound? One surface bends it, and it is the one in focus.**
`docs/design/mocks/shared/UX-PATTERNS.md` already carries the whole vocabulary
for time and has for a while: section 1 states the rule as "timeline when time"
(the timeline dock and transport bar appear only when the document has time),
lists the bottom dock as region 8 with the transport above the timeline, and
gives the responsive behaviour for both. D8 settles what may sit on the
transport row and where everything else goes instead. D10 settles that a
property list shows what can be animated rather than what is. Against that, the
app as shipped opens a recording in its own editor window with its own trim
strip and its own controls, which is the one shape section 1 forbids. So the
finding is not a missing rule, it is a shipped surface that predates the rule
and never came back to it. Both corrections are already queued and already first:
`refine-the-video-design-so-it-reads-as-the-same` (p1, seq 10) and
`a-document-can-have-time` (p1, seq 20). The citations above were folded into
the design task today so it reconciles the fifteen video clickthroughs against
the rules that exist rather than deriving a second set.

**Are we missing components or guidance?** Nothing was hand-rolled this cycle
worth extracting: the three finished tasks were core logic, a naming rule and
loop plumbing, with no new UI. The extraction risk is all ahead of us. Video
brings a transport, a timeline with lanes, waveforms, channel strips, transition
settings and caption lists, and there are fifteen clickthrough pages that each
drew their own. `comp-video.html` exists precisely to hold the time primitives
and says so out loud ("they look like decoration, they are not"). The design
task's acceptance already requires the primitives be drawn once there and reused,
so this is covered rather than newly filed.

**What UX issues keep recurring? Features shipped that nobody has seen.** This is
the pattern of the week and it is in the audits' own words, four in a row, all
video-cutting:

- `2026-09-15-video-cutting.json`: "NOTHING HERE WAS PHOTOGRAPHED. The Mac's
  screen was locked for the whole of this task."
- `2026-09-16-video-cutting-trim.json`: "NOTHING HERE WAS PHOTOGRAPHED, AND
  THERE ARE NOT EVEN OFFSCREEN RENDERS... This is the second video task in a row
  that nobody has seen drawn."

  audit at all... Nobody has felt this."

  shot labelled as taken under a lock, and the one link no test can cover (the

Each of those audits was honest about it, which is the system working. What did
