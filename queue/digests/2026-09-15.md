## Design review

**Is the IA sound? No, and the document named the day it would break.** Next grew
a bottom dock today, the motion timing strip, and walked into two rules that do
not answer it.

*D9 (every dock can be pushed away, and it always says how to get back)* says a
dock leaves a visible way back on screen the whole time it is away, and that a
dock collapsed to a row states the selection. It then names this exact day: "The
app has no bottom dock yet, so the row half of this rule is not yet owed; the day
Next grows a timeline, this is what it has to do, including the summary, and it
does not get a second collapse idiom to do it with." What shipped closes to
nothing. `MotionStripView.swift:117` is the x; `EditorView.swift:187` is
`if editorState.isMotionStripShown { MotionStripView() }`; grep finds exactly two
callers of `toggleMotionStrip`, that x and the View menu item. Three lines above
that code, the app's own comment states the principle it breaks: the side panel
got its toggle in the title bar "so the way back never moves and never goes away
with the thing it collapses." And the decision option the strip was built from
promised it outright, in pro three: "One click on its close control shrinks it to
a single row that still says what you are editing, and the canvas comes back."
Filed as `closing-the-timing-strip-leaves-no-way-back-on-t`.

*D10 (a property list shows what CAN be animated, not what is)* is now flatly
contradicted by a shipped surface. The Motion list shows only what IS animated,
with the catalogue behind the plus. That is not a slip, it is the answered model:
the user rejected a canned list of motions in as many words. But D10 was written
for a video clip inspector with keyframe diamonds, its canonical page is still
`pages/video.html`, and video has not moved. The document now carries one rule
that two shipped surfaces answer in opposite ways, with nothing saying which
applies where.

**Are we missing components or guidance? Yes, one of each, and both were queued.**

The motion work hand-rolled a third number field. `GeometryNumberField`
