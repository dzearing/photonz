## Design review

**Is the IA sound? One rule bent, four times, in four different features.**
After you do something, where should the right hand panel be looking? Nothing
written down says, so every feature this cycle answered it differently:

* After a component drop the panel scrolls itself to the Library, so the
  Component section about the thing you just placed spans -203 to -32 in a
  viewport of 841 (`queue/audits/2026-09-22-component-drag-lands-a-copy.json`).
* Writing captions selects the first caption, which scrolls the panel to it and
  takes the Captions section out of view
  (`queue/audits/2026-09-21-captions-from-the-sound.json`).
* Picking a component puts its Time section last before Library, so on a short
  window you scroll for it
  (`queue/audits/2026-09-22-components-on-the-timeline.json`).
* The full sweep fails `dock-picked-first-walk` on `sectionInView("Appearance")`
  timing out after four seconds, which is the open task
  `appearance-is-below-the-fold-again-because-the-p` caught by a walk.

Folded into `the-panel-jumps-to-the-shelf-after-a-drop-instea` and its acceptance
widened: whoever fixes the drop writes the rule into the shared patterns doc
beside the dock rules, so the fifth feature inherits it instead of guessing.
This is a rule the foundation is missing, not four bugs.

**Missing components or guidance: one real one, verified in code.** Every round
icon button in the app dims when it cannot be used, and then still brightens and
still grows its hover ring when you point at it.
`Sources/Photonz/IconActionButtonStyle.swift` reads `isEnabled` for exactly one
thing, `.opacity(isEnabled ? 1 : 0.4)` (lines 70 and 148), while `fillOpacity`
(lines 85 and 156) never consults it, so a disabled button draws the hover fill
and runs the 0.12s ease. Found on the video bar, which is the one place with
four permanently dimmed buttons side by side, but it is the shared style, so it
is everywhere. Filed as `a-button-that-cannot-be-pressed-stops-lighting-u`, p3,
because design-system is staged next and it does not unblock the focus.

**Recurring UX issue: an undo puts the document back without putting back

editing, a marquee plus Backspace went silent after two undos. That second one

## Ready to try

**Cut a recording end to end.** Record something, press Shift Command 6, and the
newest capture opens in the ordinary editor at the moment you last left it.
Press B to cut, Delete to drop a piece, and the clip's row in the Layers list
says how many pieces it is in.
Audits: `queue/audits/2026-09-22-opening-a-recording.json`,
`queue/audits/2026-09-22-video-cutting-piece-count.json`.
One caveat worth knowing before you try: delete a piece, undo, and Delete stops
working until you click elsewhere. Filed p1 this morning.

**Make the cut look intentional.** A transition sits at the cut between two
clips, a punch in pushes in and holds and pulls back out, and a stretch can be
sped up or slowed down. Audits: `2026-09-21-transitions-at-a-cut.json`,
`2026-09-21-punch-in-and-hold.json`, `2026-09-21-video-speed.json`,
`2026-09-21-hold-on-a-frame.json`.

**Put words on it, without typing them.** Open a recording with a voiceover, find
Captions under Sound, and press Write Captions: the Mac's own speech recognition
writes them, on device, no account and no network. Titles know when they are on
screen, and so do components you built.
Audits: `2026-09-21-captions-from-the-sound.json`,
`2026-09-21-a-title-has-an-in-and-an-out.json`,
`2026-09-22-components-on-the-timeline.json`.

**Send it.** Shift Command E on a recording writes a real video, and the sheet
says how big the file will be before it writes it.
Audits: `2026-09-21-video-export.json`,
`2026-09-21-recording-export-size.json`.

**Swap the window into a mode.** The chip beside the traffic lights reads
Everything; click it and pick Icon, Redline or Video, and the panel folds to
suit. Audit: `queue/audits/2026-09-22-window-modes.json`. Read its rough list
first: in a plain document Icon and Video fold nothing you can see, because the
sections they fold were not on screen anyway.
