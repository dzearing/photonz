## Design review

**Is the IA sound?** Nothing bent. Two shipped rules were found wrong and
corrected in the shared doc rather than worked around locally, which is what the
foundation is for. `MotionStrip` said "a layer row is a heading and never a bar",
true of a bell that rotates and false of a clip, and it became "a bar when it has
an in and an out, a heading when it does not", one rule doing both jobs with no
fork (`7ba6c9b6`). D18 said a split makes two layers, which would mean a row per
cut and an unusable timeline after five of them, and it became "a split adds a
piece to a row" (`72a2a75c`, `docs/design/video-surface.md`). Two features also
went out of their way to reuse rather than invent: the recording strip now
measures through the icon timing strip's ruler, and a recording leaves through
the Export sheet a picture already uses.

**Are we missing components or guidance?** Nothing was built twice this cycle.
Two pieces of guidance are missing and both are now queued:

- A guide must never draw a closed rectangle, because a closed outline is how the
  app says "this is what you picked". The icon keyline shipped as a dashed
  rectangle, read as a second selection, and had to be redrawn as a wash and four
  hairlines that run off the edges
  (`queue/audits/2026-09-20-icon-keylines-read-as-guides.json`). D16 covers over
  your work, wash or hairline, and never in the export, and says nothing about
  closed outlines. Queued: `the-guide-rule-says-that-a-guide-never-closes-a`
  (p3, design-system is staged next).
- A menu that prints a value it will not let you pick. Drag a pivot somewhere of
  your own, the Around row says Custom, and once you pick a named spot there is
  no way back to your point except dragging again or typing coordinates. New
  Frame and New Canvas print Custom for a size the same way
  (`queue/audits/2026-09-20-motion-pivot-reads-once.json`). Queued:
  `a-menu-that-says-custom-lets-you-get-back-to-cus` (p2), with one answer across
  all three places in its acceptance.

**What UX issues keep recurring?** Two patterns, one process and one product.

The process one: **the lock is now taxing the proof on every feature, not just
the sweep.** Five of the six audits written in 24 hours carry a caveat put there
by the login window. Four say their pictures were taken under a lock so colours
read dimmed and a tutorial card does not draw (`icon-keylines-read-as-guides`,
`nudge-stays-sharp`, `time-in-the-document`, `sweep-past-a-picked-path`), and the
fifth says the two walks that prove the change could not be run at all because
they open a menu (`motion-pivot-reads-once`). Folded into the task that carries
the awake card.

The product one: **the canvas has one vocabulary doing two jobs, and it showed up
twice in one day.** A closed outline means "picked", which is why a keyline drawn
as a rectangle read as a selection. A dragged box means either "pick these
layers" or "pick these points" depending on what it happens to go round, which is
the rule shipped in `a9d99413` and flagged in its own audit as a judgement the app
makes for you rather than a mode you chose. Other tools split those across two
arrows; Photonz puts both on Select and has not settled whether it should. This
belongs to `found-selection`, which is staged later, so no task was filed: by the
staging rule that work waits until the epic is staged, and this note is where it
waits.

**Judging video as a user, not a builder.** From the audits alone, someone can

well: one meaning for Save, a confirmation that names the file, a familiar Export
