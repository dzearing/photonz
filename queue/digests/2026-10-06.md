**Recurring UX issue: one question answered three ways.** A clip arriving on a spot that already holds a clip now does different things depending on how it arrived:

| How the clip arrives | What happens to what is already there |
| --- | --- |
| A dropped file (`docs/design/video-audio.md`) | It is covered, as Premiere does it |
| A paste, an Option-drag copy, or Add Media at Playhead (`2026-10-05-paste-a-clip-at-the-playhead`, `2026-10-05-option-drag-copies-a-clip`) | The new clip goes up onto a new track |
| A dragged clip, one or several (`2026-10-06-refused-drop-says-why`, `2026-10-05-several-clips-change-track`) | The drop is refused with "no room" |

Each runner chose sensibly on its own, and the Option-drag audit even asks you about its case. Together they mean someone who learns one gesture is wrong about the next. Filed **`a-clip-landing-where-another-sits-does-the-same`** (p2, video cutting), blocked on the card "When a clip lands on a spot that already holds a clip, what should happen?". I recommended option a: it covers what is there every way in, with Command to insert. That is Premiere's rule, and it is the one the dropped file already follows.
