## Ready to try
All of these are on in Next with default settings.

- **Title pages and name cards:** choose Sequence > Insert Title Page (or Insert Name Card) and pick a preset. It lands at the playhead on its own track, already animated, ready to type. Change it and use Save as Preset to keep your version. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-title-presets.json`
- **Title tiles in the Library:** open View > Library and click Comps. A Titles group leads the shelf. Double-click a tile to insert it at the playhead, or drag it onto a track. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-title-tiles.json`
- **An opening title that dissolves into the recording:** put a title page at 0:00 over a recording. The recording moves to start where the page's fade begins, and keeps following if you change that fade. Ripple Delete puts it back. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-opening-title-page.json`, `queue/audits/2026-10-04-opening-title-dissolve.json`
- **Clip fades:** right-click a video clip and choose Fade In or Fade Out. You can also drag the small diamond at either end of its bar, or set them in the Time section. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-video-clip-fades.json`
- **Transitions where clips meet:** right-click a clip or the ruler to add one. You can also drag a tile from Transitions; every join it can land on lights up. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-transitions-where-clips-meet.json`
- **Zoom while scrubbing:** drag the playhead through a zoom and the picture eases in and out at every frame. The framing box goes away as soon as the playhead moves. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-zoom-shows-while-scrubbing.json`
- **Picture first on a video clip:** right-click a video clip. Picture actions lead, and Audio is one submenu. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-video-clip-menu-leads-with-the-picture.json`
- **Detach Audio keeps your mix:** the detached sound keeps its volume, fades, gain and noise cleaning. `queue/audits/2026-10-04-detach-audio-keeps-mix.json`
- **Pick a track and delete it:** click a track's name, then press Delete. Undo brings it back. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-pick-a-track-and-delete-it.json`
- **A wider track column:** drag the right edge of the track names. The switches stay 4 pt apart at any width. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-track-column.json`
- **Click, click line:** with Line or Arrow, click, let go, then click again. `queue/audits/2026-10-04-click-click-draws-a-line.json`
- **Paint bucket:** press G and click inside lines on a picture. It fills just the enclosed area. `queue/audits/2026-10-03-bucket-flood.json`
- **History filter:** All, Screenshots and Videos switch within a frame. `queue/audits/2026-10-04-history-filter-switch-speed.json`
