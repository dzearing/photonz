## Ready to try
All of these work in Next with default settings:

- **Record, edit, save, paste.** Open a recording from history (Shift Command 6), trim the front with Q, cut with Command K, add a zoom from the Clip menu, and press Command S. The corner card says Saving, then Copied to clipboard. Paste into Teams. Open the tile again and you get the edit back, not a flattened video. Audit: `queue/audits/2026-10-07-save-an-edited-recording-into-history.json`.
- **Instant export of a cut recording.** Cut two or three fumbles out of a minute or more of recording and press Command E. The size settles in about a second, and the export takes under a second where it used to take over a minute. Audit: `queue/audits/2026-10-07-cut-only-fast-export.json`.
- **Zoom on a cropped recording.** Crop with C, right-click the clip, choose Add Zoom, draw a box round some text, and play it. What fills the picture is exactly your box. Audit: `queue/audits/2026-10-07-zoom-on-a-cropped-video.json`.
- **Premiere keys X, Shift I, Shift O and Command R.** Cut a recording twice, park the playhead in the middle piece and press X, then apostrophe to lift it out. Pick a piece and press Command R to change its speed. Audits: `queue/audits/2026-10-06-mark-clip-and-go-to-marks.json`, `queue/audits/2026-10-06-command-r-speed.json`.
- **The timeline looks like the mock.** Make a new video and look at the striped empty slots. Add a transition at a cut and look at the band. Add captions and see the Captions track sit under V1. Audits: `2026-10-06-empty-track-slot`, `2026-10-06-transition-band-reads-as-the-mock`, `2026-10-06-captions-under-the-picture`.
- **Animate a property.** Type a title, pick it, and click Animate a property. The list opens over the section, you can type straight away, and colours show swatches. Audits: `2026-10-06-animate-a-property-over-the-panel`, `2026-10-07-animate-colour-swatch`.
