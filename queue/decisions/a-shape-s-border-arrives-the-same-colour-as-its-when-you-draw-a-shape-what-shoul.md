# What a shape's first border should look like

## What this is about

Draw a rectangle or an oval in Photonz Next and it lands with two things: a
fill, and an edge. The edge is a **Border** row in the Effects list on the right,
with a colour, a position (inside, centred or outside) and a width.

The border a shape arrives with is painted the **same colour as the fill**. On a
filled shape that means there is nothing to see. The panel says Border, Inside,
4 pt, and the picture has a plain flat box.

![A fresh box today: the panel says Border 4 pt, the picture has no line](2026-09-08-border-seed-today.png)

That is a real dead end, not just a cosmetic one. Drag the **Width** slider up
and, on an inside border, nothing happens at all; on an outside border the box
simply gets bigger. Nothing tells you that the line is there and painted in
camouflage. The only way to a visible edge is to open the Border colour well
yourself and pick something different.

It is not a slip, though. A box's edge and its inside have always been the one
"shape colour", so switching the fill off leaves you a clean red outline box.
The colours match on purpose. The question is what a shape should look like the
**first** time, before you have chosen anything.

## What you are choosing

Only the starting point changes. Whatever you last left a border set to on a box
or an oval still carries to the next one you draw, exactly as it does now, and
nothing already drawn or saved changes.

### a. A deeper shade of the fill (recommended)

![A red box with a deeper red edge](2026-09-08-border-seed-deeper-shade.png)

The box is still one colour, with an edge that reads as an edge. Width does
something visible on the first drag. On a dark fill the edge goes lighter
instead of darker, so it still reads.

### b. A near-black edge, whatever the fill

![A red box with a near-black edge](2026-09-08-border-seed-dark-ink.png)

The classic outlined-shape look. Always legible, never depends on the fill, but
it puts a second colour into your picture that you did not ask for.

### c. No edge until you ask for one

![The panel with an empty Effects list](2026-09-08-border-seed-no-border-panel.png)

A fresh shape is a fill and nothing else, and the Effects list is empty until
you press the plus. The border you then add comes in a colour that stands out
from the fill.

Worth knowing: this reverses the answer you gave earlier today, when you chose
"shapes arrive with their edge already listed" over "shapes arrive bare". If
that answer was really about where the control lives rather than about wanting
an edge on every shape, this is the option that says so.

### d. Leave it as it is

The picture at the top stays the picture. Turning the fill off still gives an
outline box, and Border Width goes on doing nothing you can see on a filled one.
Picking this retires the task rather than sending it back to be built.

## Where to look

- The shapes live in the editor in Photonz Next: press **R**, drag a box, and the
  Border row is under **Effects** in the right-hand panel.
