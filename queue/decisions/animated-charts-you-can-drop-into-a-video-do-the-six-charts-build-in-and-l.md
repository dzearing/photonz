# Do the six charts build in and look the way you want?

## What this is

A video's Library now has an **Animated Charts** chip in its Catalog
(View > Library, Catalog, Animated Charts). It holds six charts: Bar Chart,
Horizontal Bars, Line Chart, Donut, Big Number and Comparison. Double click one
(or Sequence > Insert Chart) and it lands at the playhead on a Title track, like a
title page. Its numbers live in a new **Chart** section in the panel: a small table
of label, value and an optional colour per row, a Paste button for rows copied out
of a spreadsheet, a Unit (dollars, percent, K, M and so on) and Decimals.

Charts are titles, so the Title section's Theme, Font Pair, Energy, Background and
the per-part rows on the timeline all work on them.

## How each one builds in

- **Bar Chart / Horizontal Bars**: the title rises in, a baseline draws, then each bar
  grows from its foot one after another while its value counts up from 0.
- **Line Chart**: the line draws itself left to right; each point pops in as the line
  reaches it and its value counts up.
- **Donut**: the wedges sweep round one after another; the total in the middle counts up
  with them, then the key slides in.
- **Big Number**: the number counts up to its value, the label rises under it, and the
  change (+18%) pops in last.
- **Comparison**: two or three cards rise out of their foot one after another, then each
  card's number counts.

Each leaves in reverse, quicker.

## The options

- **Keep them as they are** (recommended): flat accent-coloured bars, clean labels, a slow
  moving background. A theme or a row's own colour restyles it.
- **Bolder**: gradient bars, faint grid lines, and each bar's value riding up with the top of
  its bar instead of counting in place. Closer to a motion template; busier over footage.
- **Quieter**: charts land on a still background so only the data moves.

Building does not wait on this answer; whichever you pick becomes the next pass.
