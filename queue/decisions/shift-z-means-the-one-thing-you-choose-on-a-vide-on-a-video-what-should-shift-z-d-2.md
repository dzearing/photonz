# What Shift Z does on a video

## The surface

On a video in Photonz Next, two commands want the same key.

- **Punch In** zooms the picture in on a box you drew on it, and holds there
  (the punch-in walkthrough, `http://127.0.0.1:8791/index.html#video-zoom-wt`,
  whose command menu prints *Punch in ⇧Z*).
- **Zoom Timeline to Fit** shows the whole video across the timeline's width.
  On 2026-09-28 you asked for the timeline's zoom buttons to go, with Fit kept
  "only as a key (Shift-Z)".

A key can only do one thing. Until now the app split Shift Z by where the
keyboard was: Punch In on the picture, Fit on the timeline, and Punch In again
on the timeline whenever a box was drawn. The menu bar printed Shift Z beside
Punch In only, so the timeline's use of it was a key nobody could read.

## What it does today

Shift Z is Punch In, everywhere. The timeline fits on **backslash**, which is
Premiere's own key for it (Zoom to Sequence). Backslash is printed beside
View ▸ Zoom Timeline to Fit and beside Zoom to Fit on the timeline's right-click.

## Option A: keep it (recommended)

Nothing else is built. Premiere editors already press backslash; the mock's
key stays true.

## Option B: Shift Z fits the timeline

Final Cut's key, and the one you named. Punch In keeps its rows in the Clip menu
and on the picture's right-click, with no key, like Freeze Frame. This departs
from the key the punch-in mock prints.
