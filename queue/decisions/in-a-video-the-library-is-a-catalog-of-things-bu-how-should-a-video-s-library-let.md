# How a video's Library lets you pick a kind of thing

## What this is

When a video is open, the Library in the right-hand dock now has two pivots:
**Media** (the recordings, sounds and pictures this video holds) and
**Catalog**. Styles and Systems are gone from a video's Library; they still show
for pictures and designs.

The Catalog holds everything made for video, filed by what it is: Title Pages,
Name Cards, Backgrounds (the ten moving looks), Transitions, and Logos & Brand
(pictures this video holds). Animated Charts, Callouts and End Cards will appear
when something exists for them. Components, which are built for mockups, only
show when the **Built for video** checkbox is turned off.

The mock: http://127.0.0.1:8791/index.html#video-library

## The question

How do you pick which kind the tiles show?

### A. Chips that wrap above the tiles (what is built now)

Under the search box sits a row of small pills: `All 36`, `Title Pages 9`,
`Name Cards 3`, `Backgrounds 10`, `Transitions 12`. In the dock's usual width
they wrap to two lines. One click narrows the tiles to that kind; All shows every
kind under its own header. You can read every kind and how many there are
without opening anything.

### B. A column of kinds down the left

Like the Finder sidebar or Final Cut's title browser: a narrow list of kind
names on the left of the Library, tiles to its right. It adds no lines above
the tiles, but the dock is about 300 points wide, so the tiles would drop to one
narrow column and long names like "Logos & Brand" would be cut.

### C. One dropdown

A single pop-up above the tiles reads "All" or the kind picked. Smallest, but
you cannot see which kinds exist, or how many, until you open it, and changing
kind takes two clicks.

## What happens either way

Search runs across every kind whatever is picked, the kind you last picked is
remembered for the next video, and a tile is added by a double click or a drag
onto the timeline. Only the picker changes.
