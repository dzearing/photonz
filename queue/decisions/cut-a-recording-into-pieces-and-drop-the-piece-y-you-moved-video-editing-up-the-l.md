# Should the loop start building video editing?

## What happened

At 04:57 this morning the objectives list was reordered on the dashboard. Video
editing moved from tenth place to sixth, above redlining, and redlining moved
from second to seventh. Nothing else changed: every epic kept the stage it had,
so video editing is still marked later and redlining is still marked now.

That is the whole reason this card exists. The order of the list is how the loop
reads what matters; the stage is how it reads what to build. Those two now
disagree, and guessing which one you meant is how a week goes into the wrong
thing.

## What video editing is in Photonz today

More than nothing, and less than editing.

Record a screen and the recording lands in the capture library. Open it and you
get a player with a timeline underneath: a start handle and an end handle that
you drag inward to shorten the clip, a playhead, and a crop. Copy or save gives
you back an mp4 or a gif of what is left. That is real and it works.

What it cannot do is take something out of the middle. There is one clip and two
handles, so a cough, a wrong click or a notification halfway through means
recording the whole thing again. Every other thing people do to a screen
recording, cutting it into pieces, speeding a boring stretch up, zooming in on
something, putting a title on it, is not there at all.

## What each answer means

**Start video now.** The loop begins with the cut: play to the spot, press a
key, the clip becomes two pieces, delete the one you do not want. That is the
one piece of video editing that unlocks all the rest, because it is what turns a
recording from a single object into a list of pieces. It ships behind its own
switch in the Experiments window, so you can try it the day it lands and turn it
off if it is wrong. Icon work and the panel work keep going, more slowly.

**Sketch video first.** There are already fourteen video sketches in the design
study covering cutting, transitions, zoom, freeze, titles, captions, audio and
speed. This answer sends the loop to harden the ones that matter, so you can
click through the whole experience as pictures and say which parts are right,
before any of it is built. You can see them at
http://127.0.0.1:8791/index.html and the cutting one is
`docs/design/mocks/pages/video-cut-wt.html`.

**Ordering only.** The drag was about the long view rather than the next week.
The loop carries on with building UI, icons and redlining, and video waits until
you say so. Answering this way retires the task; nothing is lost, and video can
start any time you move it.

## The thing worth weighing

Photonz has one real user workflow today and it is redlining a screenshot. This
morning's drag put that seventh. If that was deliberate it is a bigger change
than starting video, and it is worth saying so in a sentence on the dashboard so
the loop stops spending its best slots on redlining polish. If it was a tidy-up,
say ordering only and everything carries on.
