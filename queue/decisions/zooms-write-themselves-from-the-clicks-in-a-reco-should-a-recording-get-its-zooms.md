# Zooms from your clicks

## What this is about

When you record your screen in Photonz, the recording remembers where the
pointer went and every click you made. On the timeline you can already draw a
zoom on a clip: a stretch of time where the picture moves in on a spot, and it
can follow the pointer. Today every zoom is drawn by hand, one at a time.

Screen Studio, the app many people use to turn a screen recording into
something to post, makes those zooms for you. It looks at where you clicked and
moves in on each burst of clicks, then back out. That is most of why a raw
recording comes out of it looking finished in a few minutes.

## What you would see

**Add Zooms from Clicks, one command.** Right-click a recording on the timeline,
or open the Clip menu, and choose Add Zooms from Clicks. A zoom appears on the
clip for each group of clicks. Play it back and the picture leans in just before
you click and eases out after. Each one is an ordinary zoom: drag it, trim it,
delete it. Undo takes all of them away at once.

**Zooms appear by themselves.** Open a recording that has clicks and the zooms
are already there. You turn them off in the clip's settings if you do not want
them. This is closest to Screen Studio, but it means opening a recording changes
it before you have touched anything.

**Do not build this.** Zooms stay hand drawn.

## Recommendation

One command (the first option). You get the polished look when you ask for it,
and a plain trim of a recording never changes behind your back.
