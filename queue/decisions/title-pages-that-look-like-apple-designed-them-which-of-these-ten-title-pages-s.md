# Ten title pages, studied from the people who do it best

## What this is about

A **title page** is the full-screen card that opens a video or a chapter of one:
a few words on a plain background, coming in, holding, and leaving. Photonz
offers a shelf of them in the video editor. On 2026-10-17 you said today's set,
even with its moving backgrounds, looks like a high school presentation and
would embarrass Apple.

So this round starts over, outside the app. Nothing has been built yet.

## What changed in how they are made

- **Type first.** SF Pro Display and New York, with real letter spacing (tight
  at large sizes, open on small capitals) and tight line spacing.
- **Restraint.** Black, white, off white or navy. At most one colour, and on
  most pages none.
- **Calm motion.** Entrances of 0.6 to 0.95 seconds that land softly, moves of
  10 to 22 pixels, a little blur clearing. Nothing bounces, spins, flips or
  glows for its own sake. Leaving is quicker, about 0.4 seconds.
- **Still backgrounds**, except Sessions Stage, whose reference moves, and
  there the glow drifts 60 pixels over 14 seconds.

## What each answer does

- **Build all ten, retire today's set** (recommended): the shelf becomes these
  ten. Titles already in your films keep their look.
- **Build all ten, old set under More styles**: the ten lead; the old nine wait
  in a collapsed group.
- **Not there yet**: nothing is built; your note drives the next mock.

With either build answer, **put any numbers to cut in your note** (for example
"drop 06 and 08"), and say which, if any, still look amateur.

## What happens next

The app first learns to set type this way (letter spacing, line spacing, New
York, a gradient fill), which is filed as its own task. Then each page you keep
is built to match the mock frame for frame: the audit lays the app's frames at
the same moments beside the mock's, and a second card brings you a reel of the
built set to approve.

Name cards (lower thirds) are not part of this round.
