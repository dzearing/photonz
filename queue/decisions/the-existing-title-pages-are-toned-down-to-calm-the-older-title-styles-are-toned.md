# The older title styles, toned down: are they calm now?

## What this is

After the ten Apple-level title pages landed on 2026-10-18 you said the
existing ones were far too busy and asked for them to be toned down "to make
them a little more presentable and more subtle". Those are the twelve older
styles: nine title pages (Keynote Clean, Soft Gradient Glass, Kinetic Bold,
Editorial Serif, Tech Terminal, Behind Glass, Light Leak, Gradient Glow,
Stacked Stagger) and three name cards (Split Wipe, Minimal Underline, Pill
Card). The pages sit under **More Styles** below the new ten, in the Library
catalog and in **Add Title Page**.

Each one was measured, then rebuilt to the rules of the mock you approved
([video-titles-apple](http://127.0.0.1:8791/index.html#video-titles-apple)),
while keeping what makes it itself:

- **Backgrounds** run at about a third of their old speed and strength or less,
  in one family of colours, with no grain (grain is redrawn every frame and
  read as flicker). Measured while each title holds, its background now moves a
  quarter to a ninth as much as before (Light Leak 12.8 to 1.4, Stacked
  Stagger 10.3 to 2.8, Keynote Clean 8.3 to 2.0, of 255).
- **Words** come in with one gentle move: a rise of 18 points or less, a blur
  that clears, a slide up out of their own line, a short tracking settle, or
  typing for Tech Terminal. Nothing springs, flips or turns in 3D any more.
  Each lands in under a second on a soft ease and leaves in about 0.4 s.
- **Type** is set tighter, with lighter weights for the big headlines
  (semibold rather than bold; Editorial in a regular serif).
- **Glows** are faint (Light Leak, Gradient Glow) or gone.

Titles already in your documents keep drawing exactly as they were: a title
carries its own look once it is placed.

## What still has character on purpose

- **Kinetic Bold** keeps its saturated blue: a deeper blue, but blue is the
  style.
- **Tech Terminal** keeps a slow grid, the typing and the blinking cursor.
- **Gradient Glow** keeps coloured words (now one violet to pink family) and a
  faint violet aurora with soft streaks.
- **Keynote Clean** keeps its specks of light, dimmer and slower.

## The options

- **a. Ship as toned down.** What the reel shows stays on the shelf.
- **b. Some are still busy.** Name them and say what bothers you; those get
  calmed further and the rest ship.
- **c. Make every background hold still.** As calm as the new ten, at the
  cost of the older styles looking much like them.
