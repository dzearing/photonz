# Ask's moving icons: rest them, or change how they are made?

## What this is about

When you ask Photonz's Ask for an animated icon ("a door that swings open and
closes again", "a cup of coffee with steam rising from it"), it draws the thing
as layers and then writes the motion by hand: every key, every pivot, every
timing. This task tried to make that motion believable for any subject by giving
Ask a written list of general moves (turn on a hinge, fold along an edge, wave,
buzz, rise and fade, and so on) and a few rules that tidy its timing.

## What was measured

Sixteen everyday animated requests were written by an agent that never saw
Ask's instructions, sealed away, and never looked at while tuning. Every attempt
was scored on all sixteen, each request run twice, so a real gain of about two
points shows and chance does not.

* Ask as it is today: **35.9 out of 100** (two readings of the same code, 35.7 and 36.0).
* Eight attempts read between 29.8 and 37.3. Only one difference was real, and it was a drop.
* The last attempt, measured the steady way, read 35.0: inside chance.

So Ask's moving icons are exactly as they were. The technique list, the sealed
requests and the steadier eval are the lasting results.

## The picture

The card's picture is Ask's door, frame by frame. In the boxed row the door
turns about its corner and pokes out past its frame, instead of swinging open
on the hinge along its edge. That is the typical failure: the model chooses a
sensible move but writes keys that do not carry it out.

To see the sealed set itself: open
`.ask-eval/held-out-runs/20261115-152626-ask-held-out-motion/contact-sheet.png`
and `ends-reel.mp4` in the same folder (the best four, then the worst four).

## The options

**A. Stop here (recommended).** Nothing changes in the app. The loop goes back
to other work. A later attempt starts from the technique list and the fair
eval.

**B. A bigger change.** Ask stops writing keys for a move. It says "this part,
this move, around this edge", and the app builds the motion from that choice,
the same way for any subject. This is several days of work and might still not
clear the noise; it would be stopped after its first step if that step reads
inside chance.

**C. More small tweaks on a bigger test.** Double the sealed set so a one-point
gain shows, and keep adjusting words and rules. Each reading takes about three
hours, and eight such tweaks have moved nothing so far.
