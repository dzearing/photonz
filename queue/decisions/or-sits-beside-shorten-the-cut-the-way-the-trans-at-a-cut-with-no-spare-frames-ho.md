# Where "Or" sits beside Shorten the cut

## What this is

When two clips meet at a cut and neither has any spare frames past its edge (a
second clip dropped at full length after a recording used to its last frame),
the cut cannot pay for a cross dissolve, a push, a wipe or a blur. The picker
greys those, and the Transition section of Properties now says
**No spare frames for an overlap** and offers one button: **Shorten the cut by
0.4s**. Clicking it trims 0.2s off each clip end, closes the gap and puts the
dissolve on, as one undo.

The transition mock draws that offer as one row: the word **Or** in the label
column on the left, a small button with a squeeze icon beside it
([mock, step 10](http://127.0.0.1:8791/pages/video-transition-wt.html)).

## The catch

At the panel's usual width the full button, icon and all, is about 25 points
too wide to sit beside the label column. The panel never cuts a button's words
off, so today it puts the button on a line of its own under **Or**.

See it: `queue/audits/2026-10-15-shorten-the-cut-1b-the-offer-sc.png`.

## The options

- **A. Keep it stacked** (what ships). Or on one line, the full button under it.
- **B. Drop the icon.** Or beside a button reading "Shorten the cut by 0.4s"
  with no icon. Fits at the usual width.
- **C. Shorter words.** Or beside a button reading "Shorten by 0.4s" with its
  icon; the full sentence is in the button's tip and the orange line above.

Whichever is picked, a narrower panel still stacks it rather than cutting words.
