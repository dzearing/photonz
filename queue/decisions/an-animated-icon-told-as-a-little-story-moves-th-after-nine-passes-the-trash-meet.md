# Story icons: stop here, or keep working on the bounce?

## What this is about

You asked Ask for: *"make an animated trash icon where the lid opens and some trash is thrown in, bouncing off the lid and causing it to close."* At first you got a still drawing. That was fixed days ago. Ask now animates every story like this, beat by beat.

This task kept going because of a stricter bar: six test stories (your trash, a bell, a checkbox, a heart, an envelope, a rocket), and five of them should read as asked, scoring 7 or more out of 10 from a judge looking at the frames. Nine passes have reached at most two of six. Your trash scores 4 to 5 in every run.

## What you see today

The boxed frames in the card's picture are your exact sentence, made in the app today (`queue/audits/2026-11-10-story-trash-bounce-two-laps.gif` is the reel, two laps):

- 0.3 s: the lid swings open.
- 0.6 to 0.9 s: the trash goes in under the lid and meets it.
- 0.9 to 1.1 s: the trash steps down off the lid into the can. **This is the bounce. It is real now, but small.**
- 1.2 to 1.35 s: the lid shuts and the trash fades inside.

## What changed in this pass

- After hitting the lid, the trash moves away from it and never back through it. Before, it sank through the lid in every run.
- When Ask plans the trash hidden until it is thrown, it now flies in from beyond the edge of the frame instead of appearing beside the lid.

## What it costs to keep going

Each pass takes about three hours of the loop. Your trash sentence comes out differently every run (Ask's model plans it fresh each time), and that spread is wider than any change has moved the score. More passes may still help. They will not be quick.

## The options

- **Stop here, keep what ships.** The task closes for good. If a story icon disappoints you later, say so and it comes back as a new task with that example.
- **Keep going, judged on your trash.** More passes aimed at your sentence first: the trash thrown in from outside every time, a clear bounce off the lid, the lid shutting because of it. Each pass ends with the reel in front of you, so you can stop it at any point.
