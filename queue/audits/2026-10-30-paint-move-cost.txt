After the 2026-10-30 evening pass (same walks, same Mac, p95 in ms per move): before the
WYSIWYG change -> this morning -> now. What changed: a dab's own pixels split across cores
at an eighth of the size a plain copy splits at (the thinned end's dabs, and a whole 150 pt
oil dab, ran on one core); a pencil's or crayon's dabs laid as one batch across cores (they
only ever keep the larger value, so order cannot matter); charcoal's and chalk's smear
split by rows; the tail's undo journal saves a pixel once rather than once per dab over it;
the overlay handed to the screen in 128 px pieces rather than 256. Same pixels byte for byte
(Paint keeps its pixels).
