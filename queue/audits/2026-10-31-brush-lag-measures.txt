What changed on 2026-10-31: a mouse stroke's end is drawn thinned as it goes, so a move
with a new dab took the old end back, laid the dab that had just gone down for good, and
laid the new end: three dabs of work on that move, and none on the moves between (a 500 pt
brush lays a dab every 50 pt, every fifth move). Now, once a move is on screen, the dabs
the next move will settle are laid in the gap before it (LiveMarker.layAhead), and the move
lays only the new end. If the next move turns out not to settle them (a release just short
of a whole spacing), they are taken back and laid the old way. Same pixels byte for byte:
BigBrushKeepsItsPixelsTests (fingerprints from before any of this work), LaidAheadTests
(every fifth move, the held and the released stroke, with and without pauses, and a wrong
guess), LiveTaperTests.
