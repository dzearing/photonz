## Where the app stands
Focus is `ui-building`, with `ui-components` and `ui-layout` holding the work.
On by default in Next today: blank canvas, typed position and size, groups,
frames, the Library, Make Component, copies that follow their original, knobs
and overrides, named colors and gradients, five starter components, align and
space, placement rules on resize, and auto layout as a stack or a grid with a
gap, room on four sides, a size of its own, and hug. Text re-measures itself
when its width or its words change.

What is missing is the top and bottom of "fits its contents": a hugging
container has no floor and no ceiling, and a stack that has been given a size
can only hold one fixed gap, so a nav bar is still built by nudging. On the
component side, a knob can still only be wording, show or hide, and a choice:
there is no way to say "this copy is the red one".

`measure-redline` is effectively finished. Hover to measure, the measurements
panel, alignment checks, arrow captions and spec export have all landed and been
audited; only "witness the capture dim in another tool" is open, and it is
waiting on an unlocked Mac.

## Findings

### Objective gap
- A hugging container has no smallest or largest size:
  `GroupLayout.swift` holds kind, direction, columns, gap, rowGap, padding,
  width and height and nothing else, so a button with one letter is as narrow as
  one letter. Named in the rough list of `2026-09-04-ui-hug.json`. Task
  `a-container-can-be-told-the-smallest-and-largest` (p1).
- A knob can only be three things. `ComponentProperties.swift:28` holds `text`,
  `visible` and `variant`, and `docs/design/ui-building.md:552` lists numbers and
  colors as a deliberate first-version limit. The epic promises "create once,
  reuse everywhere, override safely", and recoloring one copy is the first thing
  anyone tries. Tasks `a-copy-can-be-given-its-own-color` (p1) and
  `a-knob-can-be-a-number` (p3, deps on it).
- A stack cannot spread its leftover room, so the starter nav bar still holds the
  size it was drawn at. Task `a-row-can-push-its-contents-to-its-two-ends` (p2).
- Everything shipped since the last pass carries an audit with a real window
  capture. Nothing is on with no way to try it.
