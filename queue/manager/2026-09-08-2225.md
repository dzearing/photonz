## Where the app stands

Focus is `ui-building`, held by `ui-components` and `ui-layout`. The ladder the
spec set out (containment, the shelf, reuse, useful on arrival, the whole
scenario) is landed end to end, and its own "Done when" paragraph is satisfied:
draw a control, make it a component, drop copies, edit the original, watch them
follow, and the redlining flow is untouched. Auto layout has stacks, grids,
gaps, room on four sides, a size of its own, hug, smallest and largest, spread,
fill the room left over, clipping and a surface.

So the honest reading is that the focus is not out of work, it is out of the
work the spec wrote down. What remains are the limits the spec named on purpose
and then never revisited: styles are colour only, components live in one
document, a row cannot wrap, a shape rounds all four corners together. Three of
those are filed here as capability, one behind a card.

The other thing worth saying plainly: the queue has drifted into panel polish
again, harder than in September. Of the last forty finished tasks, nearly all
are one-line wording, notice and control fixes. That is audits feeding the loop
what the last change touched, which is exactly what the 2026-09-04 pass called
out and corrected by ordering. It has come back, so the capability tasks are
again at the top of both priorities.

## Findings

### Objective gap
- **A style can only be a colour.** The spec's own definition is "a named paint,
  effect or text treatment"; `ColorStyles.swift` is the whole system and every
  type in it is about colour. `TextStyles.swift` is the tool's current settings,
  not a saved style. A design system without a text style is not one. Task
  `a-text-treatment-can-be-saved-as-a-named-style` (p1).
- **A component is stuck in the document it was made in.** Named as a
  deliberate limit in the spec, and an open thread since 2026-09-04 with nothing
  filed. The epic is titled "create once, reuse everywhere". Task
  `a-component-made-in-one-document-can-be-used-in` (p2, blocked on its card).
- **A row cannot wrap.** `GroupLayoutKind` is stack or grid and nothing else,
  and there is no `wrap` anywhere in `PhotonzCore`. A grid wraps at a fixed
  column count with equal cells, which is the wrong shape for chips and tags.
  Task `a-row-that-runs-out-of-room-wraps-onto-the-next` (p2).
