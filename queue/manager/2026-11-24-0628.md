## Findings
### Objective gap
- **The frame rate is fixed at 30, and the recordings are 60.** Success line 2 says playback runs "at the document rate (60 fps where the source is)".
  - `MovieRef.framesPerSecond = 30` and `DocumentVideoExport.movieFPS = 30`. The comment on the second one says 30 is finer than any recording the app makes, but `ScreenRecorder.fps = 60`.
  - I reproduced it on the probe by exporting 5 s of the reference edit:
    - Source by ffprobe: `r_frame_rate=60/1 nb_frames=3600 duration=60`.
    - Export: `r_frame_rate=30/1 nb_frames=150 duration=5.0`.
  - The speed walk counts 1817 frames due a minute, which is 30 a second.
  - Filed in two parts: `a-60-frame-a-second-recording-edits-and-exports` (the model, frame stepping and export), then `a-60-frame-a-second-edit-plays-every-one-of-its` (playback).
- **Nothing compares playback with export across a whole edit** (success line 8). Only the jump-back task and the 50 track task compare shown frames with renders, and each in its own case. Filed `every-frame-the-whole-reference-edit-plays-is-th`. It waits on the jump-back task so it reuses that task's frame capture.
- **"Sound never stutters" is not really measured.** The speed walk looks once a second (`looks: 60, silentLooks: 0` in `short-6.json`), so a dropout of a few milliseconds is never seen. Filed `the-sound-of-the-whole-reference-edit-plays-with` at p2 as measure-first, since nobody has reported a stutter.
- **Success lines already met:** the reference edit, export time, memory and long captioned playback (tasks done 11-13 to 11-17, audits 2026-11-16-*). Everything else in the success list has an open task.
