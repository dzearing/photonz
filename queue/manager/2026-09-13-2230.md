whole first leg of the icon path the user asked for: a path can be reshaped after
it is drawn, a rectangle turns into a path, a Pen point lands on the grid line,
an icon comes out as an SVG file, and New Frame offers icon sizes 16 through 512.

keylines, and the first mark you make on a 24 pixel canvas arrives four pixels
thick. Both are filed this pass, at the front of the queue.
