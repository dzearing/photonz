## Where the app stands
Focus is `video-editing`. Every video feature (cut, trim tool, transitions,
speed, hold, punch in, titles, captions, sound as a layer, export) lives in the
editor behind `next-a-recording-is-a-document`, and that switch is **off** by
default in Next. So a person running Next with default settings still gets the
old play-and-shorten window. `a-recording-opens-in-the-video-editor-for-real-i`
(p1 seq 20) turns it on. After that come the mocks restored, then the mock's
timeline, tracks, several clips, audio segments, right-click menus, Premiere's
keys, transition tiles, motion, captions and the Premiere gap audit, in that
order. Playback of a full-size Retina recording flickers
(`playing-a-recording-never-blinks`, p1 seq 10).

## Findings

### Objective gap
- Every item on every video success list maps to an open task. Checked one by one: tracks, drag between tracks and snapping,
  blade/ripple/JKL/IO/zoom, right-click menus, transition tiles, A-to-B motion,
  speed and freeze as a dropdown and a right-click action, T over video onto a
  titles track, in and out presets, audio segments with waveform, detach, fade,
  volume, music, captions with SRT and burn-in, flicker, reachable at defaults.
  Title stroke is already there: a border on text outlines the letters
  (`DocumentRenderer.swift:847`). Export of MP4, GIF, HEIC and a single frame
  goes through the one sheet with a size shown. No action.
- **Turning the editor on by default would lose people's work.** A recording
  document reports `nothingToSave` (`EditorState.swift:1450`), and
  `nothingToSave.asksBeforeClosing` is false (`SaveAffordance.swift:54`). So once
  the switch is on, anyone who cuts or titles a recording and closes the window
  loses the whole edit without being asked. The runner who built it wrote this down on
  2026-09-20, and it is the one hole in today's plan. Folded into
  `a-recording-opens-in-the-video-editor-for-real-i` as an acceptance item: the
  close sheet appears. That is right whichever way the Command S card is answered, so the
  task still does not wait on the card.

### Competitors
- **Screen Studio, ScreenFlow and CleanShot all keep the pointer and the clicks
  separate from the picture. Photonz burns them into the pixels**
  (`ScreenRecorder.swift:59`, nothing in `Capture/` records a click). No click
  rings, no following the clicks, no smoothing or hiding the pointer. It is the
  one screen-recording gap Premiere does not cover either, and it has a deadline
  the rest do not: a recording made today can never gain it. Two previous passes
  deferred it while nine cards were pending. One is pending now, so it becomes
  a card: `a-recording-remembers-where-the-pointer-went-and`, blocked, p2 seq 5,
  behind every p1.
- Premiere parity itself is owned by `measure-the-video-editor-against-premiere-and-fi`. No
  duplicate filed.
