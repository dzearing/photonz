## Findings

### Objective gap
- **Rotation ships without a number.** `LayerTransform` holds a rotation, the
  canvas has a knob (`CanvasPointerDrags.swift:349`), and the renderer
  composites it (`DocumentRenderer.swift:858`). Nothing reads the angle back:
  `GeometryInspector.swift` has X, Y, W and H and no angle, and grep finds no
  Rotation row anywhere in `Sources/Photonz`. You can turn a thing and never
  learn what you turned it to, or get back to straight except by undo. Task
  `the-angle-you-turned-something-to-is-a-number-yo` (p1).
- **The panel contradicts itself on a copy.** Real capture: Appearance reads
  Corner Radius 0 px with its slider at the far left, the Component section
  reads Corner radius 18, and the three buttons on the canvas are visibly
  rounded. Both numbers are probably true of different layers; that is the bug,
  not the excuse. Task `two-rows-called-corner-radius-on-one-copy-hold-d` (p1,
  top of the queue).
- **The spec's "nothing rotates" line is stale**, and so is its note that
  pixel scale auto-detect is a later follow-up: `EditorState.swift:666` reads
  the DPI off the capture and sets it. No action, recorded so nobody re-files
  either as a gap.

### Competitors
- Against CleanShot X and Shottr, one thing is missing that a person doing this
  job needs every week: **you cannot cover anything in a picture**. Blur is an
  effect on a layer, so it softens the shape you drew rather than what is under
  it, and grep finds no pixelate, redact or spotlight anywhere. A redline with a
  real name or email in it has to be finished in another app. Task
  `hide-a-name-or-an-email-in-a-screenshot-before-y` (p2, blocked on its card).
  Cheap to build: the renderer already hands a layer the composite below it
  (`DocumentRenderer.swift:647`), which is how a zoom callout stays live.
- Against Figma and Sketch, a piece cannot be floated in front of a row that
  arranges itself, only behind it as the surface. Task
  `a-badge-can-sit-on-the-corner-of-a-card-inside-a` (p2, blocked on its card).
- Step numbers, a spotlight and scrolling capture are all genuinely absent and
  deliberately not filed: two cards on new visible things is already the limit
  of what is fair to put in front of the user in one pass.
