### Architecture
- **A shape you turn loses its points, and the refusal is one clause.**
  `CanvasPathEdit.swift:116` requires `layer.transform.isIdentity` before a path
  shows anything, and the comment above it explains honestly why: a point dragged
  on a turned path would swing the whole shape round, because a turn is measured
  about the middle of the box reshaping moves. That is a real problem and the
  refusal was the right first answer; it is the wrong permanent one for an epic
  whose whole job is drawing elaborate glyphs out of angled pieces. Filed:
  `a-shape-you-turned-still-shows-its-points`.
- Otherwise clean. No `@preconcurrency`. Nothing forked in `Releases/Next/`, so
  no Current change is waiting to be carried across. The same three guarded force
  unwraps in `PhotonzCore` and `PhotonzRender` as last pass; still none dangerous,
  still an open thread.
